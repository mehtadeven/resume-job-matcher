Resume–Job Matcher

Applicant-tracking systems screen resumes by keywords before a person reads them. Paste your resume and a job post to see that screening: a match score, and the exact keywords you're missing.

How it works

The same four steps as matcher.py, ported line by line to JavaScript.

  1. Rank the job's terms. TF-IDF weights every word in the job post; filler words are dropped.
  2. Keep the top 15. The highest-weighted terms are the keywords a screener looks for.
  3. Check the resume. Each keyword is marked as found or missing.
  4. Score the overlap. Cosine similarity of the two TF-IDF vectors gives a second, overall score.

Python version: python matcher.py sample_resume.txt sample_job.txt. The sample scores 46.7% (7 of 15) in both versions.
